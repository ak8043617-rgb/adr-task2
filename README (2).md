### 5. Additional Information
- **Concomitant medicine:** Paracetamol, taken as needed. No change in use reported.
- **Action taken:** Amoxicillin withdrawn; patient referred to the physician for review and symptomatic treatment.
- **Causality assessment:** Naranjo score 3 = **Possible**; WHO-UMC = **Possible**. It becomes Probable if the reaction resolves after the drug is stopped and no other cause is found.
- **Type of reaction:** Type B (hypersensitivity); nausea may be a dose-related Type A effect.
- **Relevant history:** Not reported in the scenario (known allergies and previous drug reactions to be confirmed).
- **Recommendation:** Record "suspected amoxicillin/penicillin allergy" in the patient's file and avoid re-exposure unless advised by a doctor.
- **Report submitted to:** National pharmacovigilance centre (PvPI / CDSCO in India).

## Reporting process followed
1. **Identify** the reaction and the suspected drug.
2. **Document** patient, drug, reaction and reporter details.
3. **Submit** the form to the pharmacovigilance centre.
4. **Analyze** causality and severity so the signal can improve medicine safety.
